A

partition behaviour between the mobile and the stationary phases.
क्रोोमैटोग्रााफी तब होती है जब अर्क या किसी अन्य मिश्रण में मौजूद विभिन्न विश्लेषणों में मोबाइल और स्थिर चरणों के बीच समान विभाजन व्यवहार होता है।

Analytes with high solubility in the eluent will wander fast through the column and will soon be eluted from its outlet.

C
Those analytes with high “adsorptivity” (in a broad sense) to the stationary phase will stay longer within the column
and will be eluted late.

D
